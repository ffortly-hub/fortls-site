About FORTLS

FORTLS.com is an independent hub of free tools and curated links for Fortran developers, with a focus on the Fortran Language Server ecosystem.

Independence

This site is not the official fortls project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or maintained by its maintainers or the Fortran-lang community. Names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. The official project is on GitHub.

How it’s funded

Voluntary support, labelled affiliate links and, in future, labelled sponsorships. Editorial content does not depend on them.
