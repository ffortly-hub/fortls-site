Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-22

FORTLS.com is run by its owner, reachable at hello@fortls.com. We keep data collection minimal.

What we don’t collect

No accounts, no forms, no comments. The tools run in your browser; the text you enter is not sent to us.

Cloudflare

The site is served by Cloudflare (hosting, DNS and security), which may process technical data such as IP address and request details under its own privacy policy. Email sent to @fortls.com addresses is handled by Cloudflare Email Routing, forwarded to the owner’s mailbox and used only to reply.

Analytics (only with your consent)

A single banner asks whether we may use Google Tag (Google Tag Manager), Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. Nothing from these services loads until you click Accept; Google Consent Mode v2 is set to “denied” by default. If you accept, they collect pages viewed, clicks on key links and buttons (for example affiliate, newsletter, support, download and tool copy/download events), device and browser information, approximate location, and, for Clarity, session recordings and heatmaps with sensitive content masked. They set the cookies _ga, _ga_*, _clck and _clsk. Google and Microsoft process this data under the Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement, and it may be transferred outside your country, including to the United States. We do not use analytics for advertising or personalisation.

Withdrawing consent: click “Privacy choices” in the footer. This revokes your consent, deletes those cookies and shows the banner again. Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage and is asked again after about a year.

Newsletter

Subscription is handled by beehiiv. Your email address is submitted to beehiiv, not to this site, under beehiiv’s privacy policy.

Support platforms

GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi and PayPal are operated by third parties. Payments and supporter details are handled by them under their own policies; we do not receive card details.

Affiliate partners

Links to Cloudways and Vultr go through a redirect on this site (/go/…), which does not store personal data, and then to the partner’s site. The partner may set cookies or track referrals, and we may earn a commission. See the affiliate disclosure.

Other links

Links to the Afternic/GoDaddy domain marketplace, GitHub, social platforms and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

Legal bases and your rights

We rely on your consent for analytics and on legitimate interests for hosting and security. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Email hello@fortls.com. We don’t knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this page; the date above shows the latest version.
