Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-15

FORTLS.com is run by its owner, reachable at hello@fortls.com. We keep data collection minimal.

What we don’t collect

No accounts, no forms, no comments. The tools run in your browser; the text you enter is not sent to us.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, which may process technical data such as IP address and request details to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy policy.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept in the banner, we load Microsoft Clarity, which records how pages are used (clicks, scrolling, device and approximate location, and session replays with sensitive content masked) and sets cookies. Data is processed by Microsoft under its privacy statement. If you decline, Clarity is not loaded. Use “Privacy choices” in the footer to change your decision; choose Decline and clear this site’s cookies to remove existing ones. Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage.

Third-party links

Links to beehiiv (newsletter), GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi and PayPal (support), social platforms, GitHub, and affiliate partners lead to services with their own privacy policies. Information you give them is theirs to process, not ours.

Email

Mail to @fortls.com addresses is forwarded to the owner’s mailbox and used only to reply.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Email hello@fortls.com. We don’t knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this page; the date above shows the latest version.
